## 🌸 Project Overview
This repository contains the performance analysis for **Maven Fuzzy Factory**, built as part of an intensive **Data Analytics Hackathon**. The project dives deep into 3 years of e-commerce data to uncover growth stories, optimize conversion funnels, and identify strategic opportunities for revenue enhancement using a beautiful, pastel-themed reporting structure.

<br/>

## 🧸 Dataset Details
The analysis leverages the Maven Fuzzy Factory database, utilizing highly reliable data across multiple tables (`orders.csv`, `order_items.csv`, `order_item_refunds.csv`, `products.csv`). 
* **Data Quality:** The dataset contains over 1.7M+ records with absolutely zero issues, making it fully clean and reliable for verifiable metrics [cite: 4].

<br/>

## 🛍️ Main Insights & Analytical Focus

### 1. 📈 The Growth Story & Efficiency
* Over a 3-year period, sessions multiplied by 34x and orders by 90x, culminating in $1.94M in total revenue [cite: 4]. 
* Conversion Rate (CVR) nearly tripled to 8.44%, while Revenue Per Session (RPS) grew to $5.30 [cite: 4].
* Visitor value is currently 3.4x higher than it was at launch [cite: 4].

### 2. 🚦 Channel Mix & Resilience
* While `gsearch nonbrand` currently drives 60% of sessions (representing a single point of failure), free traffic successfully grew to 35% by 2015 [cite: 4]. 
* Approximately one-third of the revenue now survives without paid ads, indicating strong brand equity [cite: 4].

### 3.  funnel Conversion Funnel & A/B Testing
* **The Leak:** The biggest revenue leak occurs on product pages, which see a 55% drop-off rate [cite: 4].
* **Landing Page Wins:** The `/lander-1` A/B test lifted conversion by 0.87 percentage points, adding $3.8K/month in revenue [cite: 4].
* **Billing Page Wins:** The `/billing-2` experiment was the highest-value test, lifting revenue by 37% and generating an additional $27.5K/month [cite: 4].

### 4. 📦 Product Margins & Cross-Selling
* **Product Mix:** The "Mr. Fuzzy" product accounts for 62% of revenue but yields the lowest margin, whereas "Sugar Panda" provides a high margin [cite: 4].
* **Cross-Sell Strategy:** The "Mini Bear" product has a 21% attach rate across all products; making it the sole cross-sell option at checkout could lift attachment to 28% (an estimated $106K/year value) [cite: 4].
* **Refund Crisis:** A major refund spike was identified and resolved in under 30 days by switching to a new supplier, preventing structural issues [cite: 4].

### 5. 💝 Customer Loyalty
* Repeat users are 2.42x more valuable than new customers [cite: 4]. 
* Despite this high value, there is zero email capture in place, highlighting a massive opportunity for CRM implementation [cite: 4].
